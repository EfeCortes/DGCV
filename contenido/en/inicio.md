## Misión
The Graphic Design and Visual Communication program is an academic unit of higher education within the Faculty of Architecture and Habitat Sciences. It trains students with professional ethics, human values, and a technological and scientific foundation that contributes to identifying and solving visual communication problems in society as a whole.

## Visión
The Graphic Design and Visual Communication program is recognized as a national and international reference in the education of research professionals with academic and scientific quality, critical thinking, professional ethics, and commitment to regional and national development.

## Objetivos
To train professionals in graphic design and visual communication who are capable of providing communication solutions for the diverse needs of society.
