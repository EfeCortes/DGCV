## Introducción
Visual communication is power. We teach how to manage high-impact campaigns, complex data visualization, and narrative, preparing graduates for leadership roles in agencies.

## Prácticas preprofesionales
The program promotes pre-professional practices as a fundamental part of academic education. They take place both within courses and in external spaces to strengthen the link between theory, practice, social context, and professional work. Through institutional agreements and university regulations, students participate in real projects and respond to concrete needs from institutions and organizations.

## Convenios
The program has agreements with institutions inside and outside the university, allowing students to join real professional environments and develop technical, creative, and management skills.

## Relevancia prácticas
Pre-professional practices allow students to face real work situations, understand professional dynamics, and develop their own criteria for design decision-making. They also strengthen communication, teamwork, responsibility, portfolio building, and professional insertion.

## Laboratorios MyClub
The laboratories are oriented toward applied design practice. Their goal is to create a space where students transform ideas into physical and tangible products through experience. Design education requires experimentation and real production, using equipment such as screen-printing machines, 3D printers, and sublimation presses.

## Relevancia laboratorio
The laboratory bridges theory and practice, preparing students for professional work through experimentation, production, and applied design solutions.
