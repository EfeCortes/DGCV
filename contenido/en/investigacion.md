# Research

## Etiqueta
Research

## Titular
Research is also design.

## Introducción
Research strengthens critical thinking, academic production, and the ability to respond to social issues through design and visual communication.

## Investigación formal
Formal research is led by CIIDIS. The program develops research through three lines that guide students' academic work. In Design Studio III, coordinated by Arq. Jaime Alzérreca, these lines address problems related to design and visual communication, promoting research training and social engagement. Results are presented every year through a poster that shows contributions to academic education, research, and social impact.

## Investigación formativa
Formative research focuses on learning the knowledge and skills required to investigate. Each course helps students acquire tools for functional research.

## Habilidades básicas
Logical and intellectual processes to identify, analyze, discuss, and communicate in an organized way.

## Construcción conceptual
Skills for recognizing concepts, paradigms, approaches, and research methodologies; appropriating and reconstructing ideas; generating personal positions; and defending them.

## Metodología
Practice in problem formulation and the use of tools for information gathering, data analysis, interpretation, and knowledge generation.

## Producción académica
Development of different types and formats of academic work, using ethical citation and reference standards to share knowledge.

## Habilidades digitales
Use of digital tools for searching and organizing information, selecting reliable sources, using databases and reference managers, evaluating information, and reusing networked information.
