# Undergraduate Program

## Etiqueta
Formation

## Titular
Design the visual revolution.

## Introducción
The Graphic Design and Visual Communication program at UMSS is grounded in an academic, social, and research-oriented approach. It trains professionals capable of understanding visual communication from cultural, social, and regional perspectives. Students develop critical thinking and a strong artistic foundation through the guidance of qualified professors committed to integral education.

## Formación
We train creative minds capable of transforming abstract concepts into powerful realities. Your vision is the tool; our university is the impulse.

## Admisión PSA
The program admits students through the Academic Proficiency Test (PSA), an entrance exam that evaluates knowledge in areas such as history, language, geometry, mathematics, and general culture.

## Admisión preuniversitario
The program offers pre-university preparatory courses as an admission pathway. These courses strengthen knowledge in language, geometry, general culture, and related areas, allowing direct admission after satisfactory completion.

## Admisión especial
Special admission is intended for outstanding students, university transfers, or applicants entering through specific agreements, according to academic requirements and university regulations.

## Requisitos
Common general requirements: high school diploma, registration and payment, identification document.

## Docentes
The program has professors linked to studios, theory, technology, communication, drawing, history, methodology, management, and degree completion options.

## Adscripción
This modality allows students to obtain their degree through practical work inside the university. After completing all courses, the student applies to an internal call to develop a real project related to academic, research, outreach, or university management activities, responding to a concrete institutional need with evaluable results.

## Trabajo dirigido
Directed Work consists of specialized and supervised practical work in public, private, or mixed institutions or companies with a valid agreement with UMSS. The student solves specific visual communication problems through advising or consulting, under institutional and methodological supervision, and presents a final report.

## Diplomado
Double degree, Diploma is based on completing a complementary higher education program that deepens knowledge in a specific design area. It includes theoretical and practical modules, attendance, participation, evaluations, and a final integrative project.

## Tesis
The thesis is an original research project required to obtain the undergraduate degree in Graphic Design and Visual Communication. It is developed with a tutor and reader, then defended before a jury.

## Proyecto de grado
The Degree Project is a research and design project developed in Design Studio V. It integrates theoretical and practical knowledge to solve a visual communication problem through a grounded graphic proposal, with academic follow-up and oral defense.
