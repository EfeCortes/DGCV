# Graduate Studies

## Etiqueta
Graduate Studies

## Titular
Continuing education in design.

## Descripción
All current graduate programs are offered online. There is a 10% discount for cash payment, in addition to other benefits established by specific agreements or university regulations.

## Requisitos licenciatura
Application requirements for professionals with an undergraduate degree: application letter addressed to the Director of Continuing Education, Degree and Graduate Studies, Dra. R. Martha Arévalo Bustamante, explaining motivations; identity card; academic diploma; national provision title; summarized curriculum vitae; application form; payment receipt; signed study commitment for payment plans or discounts.

## Requisitos titulación
Application requirements as a degree completion modality: acceptance request addressed to the Graphic Design and Visual Communication program director; enrollment request addressed to Continuing Education; application form; updated academic record showing enrollment in Degree Studio; summarized curriculum vitae; identity card; payment receipt; signed study commitment.

## Programas
Diploma in Higher Education by Competencies Applied to the Teaching of Habitat Sciences; Diploma in Higher Education by Competencies Applied to the Teaching of Habitat Sciences; Diploma in Higher Education by Competencies Applied to the Teaching of Habitat Sciences; Diploma in Higher Education by Competencies.
